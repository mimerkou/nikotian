2. What data we collect

When you send us a message through the contact form

Your full name, email, telephone and the content of your message. When you reach the form from an instrument’s page, the message is pre-filled with the model’s name and the code of its page is sent with it, so that we know which instrument you are asking about.

When you contact us by email or telephone

The details you give us yourself, so that we can reply.

When you visit the website

The hosting server automatically records technical data (IP address, browser type, date and time) for the secure operation of the website. Hosting provider: [provider]· retention period: [period].

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your request and inform you about instruments, services or a visit to the showroom (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract).
  • For the security and proper functioning of the website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: legitimate interest).
  • Where a purchase or service follows, for our accounting and tax obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

We do not send newsletters or marketing messages without your consent, and we do not sell or pass your data on to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How long we keep it

Messages and contact details are kept for [e.g. 24 months from the last contact], unless the law requires a longer period (e.g. for sales records). They are then deleted.

5. Who has access

The people at the House who handle your request, and the providers who support us technically, as processors, under contract and for this purpose only: [hosting provider], [email provider]. [If any provider is outside the European Economic Area: country and transfer safeguards.]

6. Cookies and external services

The website does not use cookies or any analytics or advertising tools, and does not store information on your device. Our fonts are hosted on our own server, with no connection to third parties.

The links to Facebook and Google Maps open those services only when you choose them; from then on, their own privacy policies apply. If tools that use cookies are added in the future, your consent will be asked for first.

7. Your rights

You can at any time ask for access to your data, its correction or erasure, restriction of processing or data portability, or object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it.

Email us at info@nikotian.gr; we will reply within one month at the latest. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr (opens in a new tab)).

8. Security

The website runs over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and access to messages is limited to the people who need them. [Additional measures, if any.]
